      FAQ

      Quick answers.

      Can I text instead of calling?

      Yes. Text 801-916-1651 any time with your address and what needs cleaning, and you'll get a reply to set up your visit. Or book a time right on this page.

      Is the instant quote my final price?

      The calculator gives you a clear starting estimate based on rooms and items. Your technician confirms everything on arrival before any work starts, so there are no surprises.

      Do I need to move furniture first?

      Just pick up small items, toys and anything fragile from the floor. Let us know about large furniture when you book and we'll plan around it.

      How long until I can walk on my carpet?

      Drying time depends on the carpet, airflow and the weather. Your technician will give you a time estimate for your home before leaving, plus tips to speed it up.

      Do you clean offices and commercial spaces?

      Yes — commercial carpet cleaning is available. Book a walk-through above and we'll quote your space and schedule around your business hours.

      Can I add my couch to a carpet appointment?

      Absolutely. Upholstery cleaning is offered as an add-on to any carpet visit — just add it in the quote calculator.
